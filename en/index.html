Remember what you read.

Chapterkeep is a memory for your reading. A few sentences after each chapter, characters and terms in a lexicon, your favorite quotes – and when you pick a book up again after a break, you know right away where you were.

Coming soon to the App Store

  • Notes per chapter

    A few sentences in your own words after each chapter – typed or dictated. Mark the passages that matter.

  • Spoiler-free recap

    After a break Chapterkeep shows you what has happened so far – only up to your chapter, even across a whole series.

  • Lexicon and character map

    Characters, places and terms with their relations – chapter by chapter, so you keep track of all the names.

  • Quotes

    Type a passage or take a photo of the page – the text recognition runs on your device. Share it as a beautiful card.

  • Flash cards

    Review what you want to keep – especially from nonfiction. If you like, Chapterkeep reminds you when cards are due.

  • Offline and private

    Works without a connection. No ads, no selling of your data. Use Chapterkeep as a guest if you like, without any contact details.

For fiction and nonfiction

Reading sessions with a timer, your own shelves, series, your reading year in cards and an export of your notes. Questions? See the help or write to contact@chapterkeep.de.
